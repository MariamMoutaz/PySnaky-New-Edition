About PySnaky

Why it exists

Most "learn to code" sites make you read for ten minutes before you touch a keyboard. PySnaky flips that — short explanations, then code you run immediately, so the concept lands through your hands, not just your eyes.

How it's built

A lightweight in-browser Python simulator handles variables, arithmetic, strings, conditionals, loops, lists, and functions well enough to give real, correct output for every example on this site — no server, no install.
